# Report template

Use this structure exactly.

```markdown
## Launch risk review: <product> (<date of information reviewed>)

**Overall status:** <On track | At risk | Off track>
<One sentence explaining the status.>

| Risk | Severity | Evidence | Recommended next action | Suggested owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <short description> | High | <quote or IDs> | <specific action this week> | <name or "Unassigned"> |

### Missing information
- <what's missing and why it matters, or "None">
```

Status rules:

- **Off track:** two or more High risks.
- **At risk:** exactly one High risk, or three or more Medium risks.
- **On track:** anything else.

Sort the table High, then Medium, then Low.
